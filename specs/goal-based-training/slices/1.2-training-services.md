# Slice 1.2: Program, schedule, and outcome services

## Objective

Expose authenticated business operations that turn a profile and program into a
dated schedule, log required/extra sets, derive workout status, and finalize day
participation without silently rewriting history.

## Signal

**Signal Skill:** None

**Expected Behavior:**
- Service and handler tests cover ownership, invalid input, idempotent replay,
  goal changes, per-date edits, status derivation, off-plan participation, and
  rejected historical deletes.
- `go test ./internal/service ./internal/handler ./internal/router` passes.

## HTTP capabilities

- Training profile: read/update one primary goal and constraints.
- Starter programs: list/filter static versioned starters and clone one into a
  user-owned editable program; allow custom program creation.
- Programs: read/update owned copies without mutating starter definitions.
- Schedule: read a date range, generate/materialize one week (or a requested
  farther range), edit one dated snapshot, and regenerate only unstarted future
  workouts after an accepted goal change.
- Workout logging: idempotently check/edit required sets, append extra sets, and
  explicitly complete an active workout.
- Day outcomes/stats: expose distinct scheduled status and participation streak.
- Exact routes follow the slice 1.1 contract: `GET|PUT /training-profile`,
  `GET /starter-programs`, `GET|POST /programs`,
  `POST /programs/from-starter`, `GET|PUT /programs/{id}`,
  `GET /schedule?from=&to=`, `POST /schedule/materialize`,
  `POST /schedule/regenerate` (preview then explicit apply),
  `PATCH /scheduled-workouts/{id}`,
  `PUT /scheduled-workouts/{id}/sets/{set_id}`,
  `POST /scheduled-workouts/{id}/extra-sets`,
  `POST /scheduled-workouts/{id}/complete`,
  `GET|POST /workout-sessions`, `GET|PATCH /workout-sessions/{id}`, and
  `GET /participation?from=&to=`.
- Starter selection guarantees at least one deterministic candidate for each of
  six goals at representative 2/3/4/5-day availability, adapting exercises to
  equipment, experience and duration. One-day availability is an honest reduced
  full-body option; when possible, major muscle groups appear twice weekly.

## Deterministic rules

1. Before finalization, zero checks is `planned` and some checks is `in_progress`;
   all required checks may finalize `completed`. Explicit completion or local-day
   closure finalizes all=`completed`, some=`incomplete`, none=`missed`.
2. Extra sets count toward history/volume/records but never required completion.
3. Any performed set on a scheduled day preserves participation; zero performed
   sets resets it. Rest/unscheduled days are neutral.
4. Completing early derives status at that instant. Transactional lazy
   finalization runs before every schedule, participation, and stats read and
   relevant mutation; it closes passed local dates using the persisted IANA
   timezone and is safe to repeat without a client completion call. A timezone
   change never rewrites finalized dates and applies only to future closure.
5. All past performed sessions (scheduled, off-plan, and legacy), past scheduled
   workouts, and finalized day outcomes cannot be deleted. A current-day
   unfinalized draft may be discarded; account deletion remains the exception.
   Historical edits may change displayed status but not finalized participation.
6. Off-plan logs do not modify the goal/program/future schedule.
7. Participation streak walks finalized scheduled opportunities newest-first,
   skips rest/unscheduled/unfinalized dates, counts participating outcomes, and
   stops at the first finalized non-participating opportunity.
8. Goal-regeneration preview/apply is atomic and refuses to replace any active
   session, including a server-known offline-active snapshot.

## Integration points

- Wire constructors through `cmd/server/main.go`, `internal/router/router.go`, and
  `internal/router/svc_wiring_test.go`.
- Reuse handler error translation in `internal/handler/response.go`.
- Keep legacy `/plan` and `/logs` behavior compatible where possible; new dated
  resources must not overload ambiguous weekday semantics.
- Existing users without a program receive an explicit adoption/setup state;
  adoption is idempotent and never silently destroys legacy plan data.
